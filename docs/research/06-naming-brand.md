# 06 — Naming & Brand Platform

> **Screening disclaimer.** Everything in the collision columns below is **informal screening only** — App Store / Google Play searches, web searches, and DNS lookups performed on 20 Aug 2026. This is **not a legal trademark clearance**. Before spending money on the identity, commission a proper clearance search in Class 9 (downloadable game software) and Class 41 (entertainment services) in the UK, EU, US, and your top-5 launch markets.

**What the screening actually proved:** the literal vocabulary of football management is exhausted. Every obvious noun — Gaffer, Dugout, Touchline, Teamsheet, Boot Room, Regista — has already been claimed by a small dev, a coaching tool, or a media brand. The only clean ground left is a word the category *speaks* constantly but has never *branded*. That word is **Silverware**.

### EA / FIFA / Football Manager clearance notes

- **No "FC" anywhere.** Not as a prefix, not as a suffix, not in the app title, not in the icon. EA Sports FC has made "FC" a live mark in this exact category.
- **Never use the phrase "Football Manager"** in the app title, subtitle, or promotional art. Sports Interactive Ltd holds a registered FOOTBALL MANAGER mark (US Reg. 5792295) covering video game software, and SEGA/SI have shown they will litigate in this category. Descriptive use inside body copy ("you are the manager of a football club") is normal-course fair use; the *title* is where the risk lives.
- **Avoid "Football Club Manager" too** — it contains the protected phrase with one word inserted. The recommended store title below deliberately uses **"Football Club Boss"**.
- No year suffixes that mimic annualised franchises (no "26", no "2027"). Nothing that reads as an FM or EA sub-brand.
- **Flagged as borderline:** any subtitle combining "Football" + "Manager" adjacently. Keep those two words apart in all metadata. If ASO pressure demands "manager" as a keyword, put it in the **keyword field** (invisible to users) rather than the title.

## PART C — BRAND PLATFORM

# RECOMMENDATION

# **SILVERWARE**

**Pronounced:** SIL-ver-ware. Three syllables, all open, no consonant clusters. Safe in every target market.

### Why this one

1. **It is the only shortlisted name with no collision inside football or gaming.** Seven of eight candidates are already occupied — one by a direct competitor shipping right now. Silverware is not.
2. **It is the category's own word, unclaimed.** Rival store listings literally ask *"how much silverware can you win?"* Commentary says it every weekend. The term is already in the player's head, carrying exactly the right meaning, and nobody has put a badge on it.
3. **It names the promise, not the plumbing.** "Manager", "Touchline", "Teamsheet" name the *job*. Silverware names the *point of the job*. Every session in this game is a step toward one thing, and the name is that thing.
4. **It is premium by construction.** Silver is weight, engraving, glass cases, ribbons. It gives the logo agent a rich, universal, non-infringing asset — an unbranded trophy, an engraved nameplate — with no club, no league, no licence.
5. **It survives the phone.** Ten characters. Displays whole on an iOS home screen and on Android. No ellipsis, no ambiguity.
6. **It is ASO-clean as a brand term.** Unique enough to rank #1 for itself immediately, and it already co-occurs with football-manager search intent.

### The honest weaknesses, and the fixes

| Weakness | Fix |
|---|---|
| In US English, "silverware" means cutlery | The store icon, screenshots, and title all say football within 200ms. Category context resolves it instantly. Use the alt title `Silverware: Soccer Club Boss` in the US storefront |
| Non-native speakers may not know the football sense | The identity carries the meaning — a trophy is universal. Localise the *subtitle*, never the name |
| Three syllables is longer than "Gaffer" | Accept it. The trade is one syllable against sole ownership of the category's best word |
| SilverWare POS exists in software | File the **stylised lockup** in Class 9 + 41, not the bare word. Commission clearance before spend |

### Positioning statement

> **Silverware is the football management career game you can play on a train — the whole job, stripped to the decisions that actually cost managers their jobs, and every one of them pointed at a trophy.**

### The promise

Not features. This:

> **You will be judged — and if you get it right, you will be remembered.**

Every football fan has quietly believed they could do the job. Silverware hands them the job, holds them to a number the board set ten months ago, and lets them find out. The reward is not a stat screen. It's the moment your name is the one on the trophy.

### Tone of voice

**The voice is DRESSING-ROOM BLUNT.**

Not broadcast-serious (too distant, too reverent, and it makes a game feel like a spreadsheet). Not dry-witty (jokes undercut jeopardy, and comedy translates worst of all). Blunt: short sentences, plain words, the consequence stated flat and left there. The dryness comes from restraint, not punchlines. It should read like an assistant manager telling you something you don't want to hear, in a corridor, quickly.

**Principle 1 — State the consequence, not the data.**
Never make the player do the arithmetic on what a number means to them.

- ✅ *"Hamstring. Six weeks. That's Anfield and both cup ties."*
- ❌ *"Player Injured. Type: Hamstring strain. Recovery: 42 days. Squad depth impact: Moderate."*

**Principle 2 — No adjective you'd be embarrassed to say out loud.**
Football people undersell. Hype is what bad mobile games do.

- ✅ *"He's good. He's not forty million good."*
- ❌ *"An electrifying world-class superstar with INCREDIBLE potential!"*

**Principle 3 — Never celebrate on the player's behalf.**
The game reports. The player feels. Confetti is allowed; exclamation marks are not.

- ✅ *"You're top. Top in November, which counts for nothing, and you'll take it anyway."*
- ❌ *"🎉 AMAZING JOB BOSS!! You're TOP OF THE LEAGUE! 🔥🔥"*

**Mechanical rules:** second person, present tense. Sentences under 12 words as the default. Sentence fragments are fine. No emoji in system copy. No exclamation marks except in quoted human speech. Never call the player "boss" more than once a session — it's a tic. Never explain the joke; there is no joke.

### Taglines

| | Tagline | Read |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Nobody remembers fifth.** | **← RECOMMENDED.** Blunt, true, funny in a way that never announces itself, and it earns the name without ever saying "trophy". Works as a hero line, an App Store banner, a t-shirt, and a loss screen |
| 2 | Fill the cabinet. | Imperative, on-brand, very good for performance ads and end-cards |

### App Store listing

**Title (30 char limit):**
```
Silverware: Football Club Boss
```
*(exactly 30 characters. US storefront alt: `Silverware: Soccer Club Boss` — 28. Deliberately avoids the adjacent phrase "Football Manager".)*

**Subtitle (30 char limit):**
```
Take the job. Win the trophy.
```
*(29 characters. Alternates to A/B: `Manage a club. Chase glory.` (27) · `One club. One shot. Go win it.` (30))*

**Description — first three lines (the only ones shown before "more"):**

```
A board with a target. A squad that isn't good enough yet. Sixteen months, if you're lucky.

Pick the team. Work the market. Face the press. Survive December.

Then do it again — better — until there's something in that cabinet with your name on it.
```

*Why these three:* line 1 sets the jeopardy with a real, specific number (average top-flight managerial tenure) instead of a boast. Line 2 is the entire game loop in nine words, so nobody has to tap "more" to understand what they're buying. Line 3 is the promise and the name, landed together. No feature bullets above the fold — features convert nobody; the job does.

### Ten in-game copy lines

1. **Board warning** — "The board met this morning. You weren't invited. They want a plan by Friday."
2. **Vote of confidence** — "The chairman has given you his full backing. Publicly. Take from that what you like."
3. **Transfer rejection (club)** — "Porto said no. Not 'no for now'. No."
4. **Transfer rejection (player)** — "He read the offer, then asked which league you're in. Wages won't fix that."
5. **Title won** — "It's done. Thirty-eight games, and the last one didn't matter. Someone go and tell them how to spell your name."
6. **Sacking** — "Thank you for your work. The car park's back through the tunnel. They rang someone on Tuesday."
7. **Youth prospect** — "Ellis Ward. Sixteen. Your academy coach says don't loan him out. He's never said that before."
8. **Injury** — "Hamstring. Six weeks. You played him Tuesday and Saturday. He knows that. So do you."
9. **Unhappy player** — "He isn't asking to leave. He's asking why he's on the bench. Answer it, or he'll ask someone else."
10. **Press conference** — "They'll ask about the referee. You can give them that, or you can give them the truth about your defence."

*Reserve lines for the same voice:* **Deadline day** — "Two hours. One phone. Nobody in Italy is answering." · **Relegation** — "Nineteenth. The maths finished weeks ago. Today it just became official." · **Promotion** — "Up. Whatever they paid you this year, it wasn't enough. Ask them tomorrow, not tonight." · **Contract offer** — "Three years on the table. Read clause seven — it's the one about finishing outside the top six."

## Handover to the logo agent

You are designing for **SILVERWARE**.

- **Wordmark:** one word, always set as one word, never "Silver Ware", never abbreviated to "SW".
- **Semantic core:** engraved metal. Silver, weight, a name cut into a plinth. The feeling of a trophy cabinet, not a confetti cannon.
- **Register:** premium, restrained, broadcast-grade. It must sit convincingly next to real football brands, not next to casual mobile games.
- **Mandatory exclusions:** no "FC" in any form. No shield/crest that reads as a real club badge. No league or federation marks. No lettering that echoes the EA Sports FC or Football Manager wordmarks. No year lockups.
- **Icon constraint:** must read at 60×60px on a home screen, in both light and dark mode, alongside 60+ other icons.
- **Name display:** "Silverware" is 10 characters and must never be truncated on the home screen — do not add a suffix to the app's display name.
- **Tone the mark must carry:** *Nobody remembers fifth.*
